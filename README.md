💎 PayLog: The Premium Financial Vault
PayLog is a high-end, professional-grade digital ledger hand-crafted for elite financial tracking. It replaces traditional paper-based Khata books with a modern, secure, and visually stunning "Masterpiece" interface. Built for power users who demand both privacy and performance.

---

 🚀 Key Masterpiece Features
1. 🏢 Dashboard Hub

* Real-Time Analytics: Instant overview of "Total Receive," "Total Pay," and your "Net Balance."
* Dynamic Visual Styles: Choose between three high-end visualizations—"Liquid Pulse", "Realistic Scale", or "Magnetic Compass"—to see your wealth ratio in motion.
* Sticky Quick Access: Instantly jump to your most frequent contacts with an anchored "See All" portal.

2. 🎙️ AI Voice Secretary

* Hands-Free Entry: Tap the mic and speak naturally: "Paid 500 to Rahul for lunch."
* Intelligence Engine: Uses advanced speech recognition to automatically categorize and record transactions without typing a single word.

3. 🏗️ Salary Architect

* Monthly Capital Planning: A dedicated zone to plan your income and fixed spending (Rent, Groceries, Savings).
* Individual Spend Tracking: Add custom manual entries for family or personal gifts.
* Surplus Analytics: A live circular gauge showing exactly what % of your capital is used vs. remaining.

4. 🛒 Shopping Ledger & Store Directory

* Partial Payment Logic: The only ledger that intelligently tracks "Unpaid", "Partial", and "Fully Paid" bills with "Remaining Due" calculations.
* Store Directory: A searchable archive for "50+ stores". View your lifetime spending at any vendor with a single tap.
* Daily Stacks: History is grouped by date into elegant, expandable cards.

 5. 🔔 Pro Notification Center

* Dedicated Alert Tray: A full-screen hub for all upcoming obligations (Lent/Borrowed).
* Contextual Redirection: Tapping a notification instantly takes you to that person's profile to settle the balance.
* Smart Cleanup: Long-press to select and clear the notification log without affecting your master records.

 6. 🧮 Financial Suite (Calculator)

* Multi-Mode Engine: Immersive "Standard", "EMI", and "Interest" (Simple/Compound) modes.
* High-Visibility Keypad: Edge-to-edge massive buttons with a professional expression preview.

---

🛡️ Security & Privacy

PayLog is "100% Offline". Your data never touches a server.

* App Lock: Secure your vault with a 4-digit PIN or "Biometric (Fingerprint/Face ID)".
* Privacy Shield: Automatically blurs app content in the Android "Recent Apps" switcher.
* Military-Grade Encryption: Security preferences and PINs are stored using "AES-256 encryption".
* Ghost Backup: Automatic midnight backups (rotating last 7 days) stored locally on your device.

---

## 🎨 Design Philosophy: Glassmorphism

* Fluid Motion: All toggles feature a "Sliding Glass Indicator" with spring physics.
* Tactile Feedback: Integrated **Haptic Vibrations** for every major action (Saving, Deleting, Swiping).
* Interactive Interaction: Uses a high-end **"Swipe to Record"** slider to confirm transactions.

---

🛠️ Technical Stack

* **Language**: 100% [Kotlin](https://kotlinlang.org/)
* **UI Framework**: [Jetpack Compose](https://developer.android.com/jetpack/compose) (Declarative UI)
* **Database**: [Room (SQLite)](https://developer.android.com/training/data-storage/room)
